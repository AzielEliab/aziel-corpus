## Two shelves
Aziel Library is the operator collection of Aziel Eliab's published work (royal purple). Corpus is the public Lamb Lens shelf. Anyone may browse both. Upload at https://www.azielcorpuslibrary.net/upload — operator session files to Aziel Library; everyone else files to Corpus.

## Read a record
Search from https://www.azielcorpuslibrary.net/ or https://www.azielcorpuslibrary.net/search. Open https://www.azielcorpuslibrary.net/record/{AZDOC-…}. The triad is always shown first. Component scores appear when applicable. See https://www.azielcorpuslibrary.net/help/how-to-read-scores.txt and https://www.azielcorpuslibrary.net/how-its-scored.

## Cite
Use the record id (AZDOC-…) plus https://www.azielcorpuslibrary.net/record/{AZDOC-…}. Machine cite: https://www.azielcorpuslibrary.net/cite.json. Person @id https://www.azieleliab.com/#aziel. Details: https://www.azielcorpuslibrary.net/help/how-to-cite.txt.
