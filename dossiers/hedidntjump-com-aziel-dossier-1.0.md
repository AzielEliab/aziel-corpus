## Identity

An Aziel Eliab Project: independent newspaper archive on Marion Zioncheck’s 7 August 1936 death in Seattle. Five research volumes, contemporary plates, and 21 inquiries of the record.

### What it is

Historical newspaper and five-volume archive. Broadsheet, official-narrative, Rubye, FOIA, and volume-reader editions.

### What it is not

- Not a software engine. Not a Softwares-tab product. Not a courtroom verdict. Does not invent quotes beyond the volumes and cited papers.
- Not a ~100-file library unpack of the source tree.
- Not a Zenodo DOI mint (deposit_needed is a separate catalog track).

Publish the contemporary record for re-reading. Challenge the official suicide account without inventing court holdings or FOIA letters.
