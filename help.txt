## Scores you will see
The triad is always the primary published score on a scored record.
TRIAD_V2 is the geometric mean of the checkers that apply to that document's concept.
SPRE, CLCE, and PhysLing appear when that component applies to the document's concept. N/A components are omitted (never shown as 0).
ZionPattern appears when the document qualifies.
Details: https://www.azielcorpuslibrary.net/help/how-to-read-scores.txt and https://www.azielcorpuslibrary.net/how-its-scored.

## Per-record LLM access points
Every record also has machine surfaces for SEO and indexing discovery:
  GET https://www.azielcorpuslibrary.net/record/{AZDOC-…}/llms.txt     text/plain positive definition of that record
  GET https://www.azielcorpuslibrary.net/record/{AZDOC-…}/cite.json    application/json structured cite
They are generated from the packed index and public metadata. They work for existing
uploads and for new uploads as soon as the record is filed. The record HTML page links
them with rel=alternate. They are listed on sitemap-records.xml.
