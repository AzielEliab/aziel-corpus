## Triad (always)
Every scored record publishes a triad first. TRIAD_V3 public combined is the 36-cycle mean: mean of all ordered pairings factor_i × factor_j among applicable factors of physics, linguistics, bayesian, truth_formula, CLCE, SPRE (36 when all six apply). geometric_mean_applicable of SPRE/CLCE/PLR is stored beside it. Display is round(combined × 100) and is never written back into combined. triad_raw freezes to the content SHA-256 at first REVIEW_SCORE. Ingest always runs V3. Stored papers remint at https://www.azielcorpuslibrary.net/v1/recalibrate-all. API: https://www.azielcorpuslibrary.net/v1/review?record_id=

## Component scores (when they apply)
SPRE, CLCE, and PhysLing stay stored for audit. They appear on the record page and cite when they apply to the document's concept. N/A components are omitted (never shown as 0).
- SPRE — provenance completeness (title, body, hash, structure, author, evidence language). Applies when the record is a filed object with provenance. physics_language and independent_source are audit flags, not silent rank boosters.
- CLCE — structural match (claims↔evidence + headings + verified-file bit). Applies when claims exist beside title or file. Token Jaccard is audit-only. SHA-256 hex is never layer P. Omit if no claims (never 0).
- PhysLing — equal-weight physics × linguistics. Applies when the document makes physics-evaluable claims (unit span or conservation/causal verb + quantity) or is energy/engineering. Bare energy / force / forensic do not qualify.
- Truth Formula — Cover-Up Truth Formula v1/v2 (ΔT / suppression / backpull) on archival/cover-up papers; documented subset on ordinary Softwares/AZDOC filings; omit if N/A.
- Bayesian — unranked likelihood of internal consistency (not world accuracy). Never a shelf sort key.

## Other published numbers
AZCoherence is a second-pass triad coherence review (PASS / FLAG / NEUTRALIZE / REFUSE).
ZionPattern Solver is a secondary public score on qualifying historical / research / investigation / crime documents.
Bayesian posterior is unranked metadata for peer review. It never sorts the shelf.
Possibility is a labeled HEURISTIC over lattice time×geo pins. API: https://www.azielcorpuslibrary.net/v1/possibility?record_id=
