## Triad (always)
Every scored record publishes a triad first: SPRE × CLCE × PhysLing as TRIAD_V1, an auditable geometric mean. Display is round(combined × 100). API: https://www.azielcorpuslibrary.net/v1/review?record_id=

## Component scores (when applicable)
SPRE, CLCE, and PhysLing stay stored for audit and appear on the record page when those verifiers have run.
- SPRE — source provenance completeness and consistency.
- CLCE — claim-to-claim consistency (AZ-CLCE).
- PhysLing — physics coherence mixed with linguistic neutrality.

## Other published numbers
AZCoherence is a second-pass triad coherence review (PASS / FLAG / NEUTRALIZE / REFUSE).
ZionPattern Solver is a secondary public score on qualifying historical / research / investigation / crime documents.
Bayesian posterior is unranked metadata for peer review. It never sorts the shelf.
Possibility is a labeled HEURISTIC over lattice time×geo pins. API: https://www.azielcorpuslibrary.net/v1/possibility?record_id=
