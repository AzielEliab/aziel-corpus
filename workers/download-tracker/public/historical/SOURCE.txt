Historical boundary sheets on the Aziel Corpus map
================================================

These four GeoJSON files are a simplified offline subset copied byte-for-byte
from AzielEliab/4dmap fourdmap/static/geo at commit
bd7295bafe03ac8bcb09f51d543f0a569fccef29:

- era-1914.geojson
- era-1945.geojson
- era-1994.geojson
- era-2010.geojson

Upstream work: André Ourednik, historical-basemaps
https://github.com/aourednik/historical-basemaps
License: GNU General Public License v3.0 (GPL-3.0)
https://www.gnu.org/licenses/gpl-3.0.html

The sheets are not relicensed under Apache-2.0. Apache-2.0 covers Aziel code
in this repository, not these four files.

What this copy is:
- Political boundaries for the sheet years 1914, 1945, 1994, and 2010 only.
- Coordinates were already rounded in the 4dmap copy. This repository does not
  move them.
- A requested year that is not one of those four is answered with the nearest
  sheet, and the response says the year is not the sheet year.

What this copy is not:
- Not a continuous atlas of every year.
- Not a surveyed boundary.
- Not topography, satellite imagery, oceanography, street map, or LiDAR.
